Privacy Policy

My IT Hero handles highly sensitive financial and identity information. We follow privacy-by-design: we collect only what is needed to prepare your worksheet.

What we collect

Your email, mobile number, a scrypt hash of your password (never the password itself), the tax documents you upload, the values you confirm, and worksheets you generate.

How we use it

Only to prepare your preparation worksheet and show you where values go on the e-Filing portal. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not submit anything to the Income Tax Department.

Security

Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies; requests are protected against cross-site request forgery; every record is tied to your account and checked on every request. Passwords, codes and tokens are never written to logs.

Your controls

From Account you can export your data, delete a document, delete a return, or delete your account and everything linked to it.

This policy has not been reviewed by a lawyer. We do not claim compliance with any specific law or regulation unless that review has been completed.

Terms of Use

My IT Hero is a preparation aid. It estimates tax and shows where information is entered on the Government of India's e-Filing portal. It does not file returns and is not affiliated with the Income Tax Department.

You are responsible for checking every value against your source documents and against the e-Filing portal before you submit your return. Do not upload documents that belong to someone else without their permission.

Draft terms — require legal review before public launch.

Tax Disclaimer

This application prepares and estimates information for tax filing. It does not constitute professional tax advice.

Calculations follow the rules stored for each Assessment Year. Rules marked “UNVERIFIED — REQUIRES REVIEW” have not yet been confirmed against official text. The e-Filing portal's computation is authoritative; if it differs from ours, rely on the portal and consult a qualified professional.

Portal labels and navigation may change. Verify the field against the current Income Tax Department e-Filing portal before submission.

Data Retention Policy

Uploaded documents are deleted automatically after the configured retention period (30 days by default) unless you choose to keep them in Account. Expired sign-in sessions and one-time codes are purged automatically.

Confirmed values in your return and generated worksheets are kept until you delete them or your account. Deleting your account removes all of it immediately.
